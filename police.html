    Serving and protecting Lego City

    The Lego City Police Department (LCPD) works with residents, businesses and visitors to keep every neighborhood safe.

    Report a concernFind a location
    In an emergency, call 911. Do not use this website or email to report a crime in progress. (Pretend site: no real calls are answered.)

    Report a concern

    Emergencies

    Crime in progress, injuries or immediate danger. Call 911.

    Non-emergency line

    Noise, parking, lost property or past incidents. Call (555) 010-0200, available 24 hours.

    Online report

    Minor theft, vandalism or lost items. Email hello@lccity.republicstates.xyz with the date, place and a description.

    Records and services

    ServiceHow it works
    Incident report copyEmail the report number and your name. Allow 5 business days.
    Background check letterBring photo ID to Headquarters. Fee applies.
    Vehicle impound releaseBring proof of ownership, insurance and photo ID.
    FingerprintingWalk in at Headquarters, Monday to Friday, 9 a.m. to 4 p.m.
    Lost and foundCall the non-emergency line. Transit items go to the Union Station booth, airport items to the FLX booth.

    Community programs

    Neighborhood Watch

    Meet your block captain and learn what to look for.

    Coffee with a Cop

    First Saturday of each month, 9 a.m. at Brick Plaza Cafe.

    Youth Safety Academy

    A summer program on bike safety, first aid and respect for others.

    Ride-along program

    Residents 18 and older can ride with an officer for a shift. Apply by email.

    Careers

    We are hiring police officers, dispatchers and records clerks. Applicants must be 21 or older, pass a background check and complete training at the LCPD Police Academy, 1200 N Main St. Send your resume to hello@lccity.republicstates.xyz.
