    Permits and building

    Apply for building, event and street-closure permits. Inspections are scheduled online or at City Hall.

    Trash and recycling

    Weekly trash and recycling pickup. Call 311 to schedule bulky item collection.

    Report a problem (311)

    Potholes, graffiti, broken streetlights and missed pickups. Dial 311 or email us.

    Business licenses

    Register a new business or renew a license at the City Clerk's office.

    Parks and recreation

    Playgrounds, the waterfront, youth sports and seasonal events.

    Utilities

    Water, power and street lighting. Report outages 24 hours a day.

    Your city government

    The City of Lego City is led by an elected mayor and a city council. The council sets the budget, passes local laws and oversees city departments. Meetings are open to the public.

    Mayor's office

    Sets city priorities and manages daily operations.

    City council

    Meets the first and third Thursday of each month at 6 p.m. at City Hall.
